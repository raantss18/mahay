**Mahay 0.1.0 (alpha)**: first release. Free, local-first creative and office apps for Linux and
Android phones and tablets, forked from ArtCraft.

> 🇫🇷 Première version. Applications libres de création et de bureautique, hors ligne, pour
> Linux et les téléphones et tablettes Android. **Alpha** : testée sur émulateurs, pas encore sur
> un vrai appareil. Voir *Known gaps*.
>
> 🇲🇬 Dikan-teny voalohany. Rindranasa malalaka hamoronana sy hiasana, tsy mila aterineto, ho
> an'ny Linux sy Android. **Alpha** izy io.

## What works

- The five apps and the hub on Android tablets, verified on an emulator (typing with the
  system keyboard, formulas, writing and saving a DOCX to Downloads and back, slides, drawing).
- Open documents from anywhere through Android's picker; saves go back to the original.
- No permissions requested on Android, no network access, no telemetry.

## Known gaps

- Not yet tested on a real phone or tablet; please report what you find.
- On phones in portrait, the ribbons of Soratra, Tabilao and Seho are cut off on the right
  (landscape works better). A compact layout is next.
- No printing, folder pickers or image clipboard on Android yet.
